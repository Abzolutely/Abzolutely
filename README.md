### 🚀 About Me

I am a **Mechatronics Engineering student** at the **Egyptian Chinese University (ECU)** with a passion for bridging physical hardware, smart kinematics, and intelligent firmware. My focus spans **autonomous robotics, sensor fusion, embedded systems (ESP32 / C++), and 3D CAD modeling**.

- 🤖 **Autonomous Robotics:** Engineered a competition **MicroMouse** maze-solving robot from scratch using an ESP32-S2 Mini, MPU6050 6-axis IMU, optical IR sensors, DRV motor drivers, and N20 gearmotors.
- ⚡ **Embedded Firmware & IoT:** Writing bare-metal and RTOS firmware for motor controllers, sensor integration (I2C / SPI / UART), and wireless communication nodes.
- 📐 **CAD & Mechanical Prototyping:** Designing chassis, mechanical assemblies, and mounting enclosures in **SolidWorks**.
- 🌟 **Leadership & IEEE:** Active leader and **IEEE Day Ambassador** at the **IEEE ECU Student Branch**, organizing technical seasons, workshops, and hackathons.
